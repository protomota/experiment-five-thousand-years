# Sources and credits

The atlas exposes per-milestone source links and image credits in **Sources & image credits**, and alongside each full reading. Keep these credits with redistributed content.

- **Wikipedia contributors:** adapted overview excerpts and abridged article readings retain source article URL, revision ID and CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution. Their license remains [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Adaptations must retain attribution and the applicable share-alike terms. The credited source revision is included in each record, rather than treating this repository as the original author.
- **Wikidata:** structured catalog facts and identifiers; see [Wikidata licensing](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing).
- **Wikimedia Commons:** photographs, paintings, portraits and artifacts have individual creator and license records in the hosted image manifest. Licenses vary; do not assume every image is public domain. Links to the original file pages remain in the app.
- **NASA Blue Marble / GIBS:** physical globe imagery and terrain tiles; [NASA GIBS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/gibs).
- **Natural Earth:** present-day boundaries and urban footprints; [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), public domain. Modern urban footprints are labeled as such, not presented as historical borders.
- **Cliopatria:** approximate historical territory reconstructions; [Seshat Global History Databank / Cliopatria](https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria), CC BY 4.0. Atlas overlays adapt these reconstructions for milestone locations and dates. Vicinity circles are atlas illustrations, not political borders.
- **Typefaces:** Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans, SIL Open Font License.
- **Software dependencies:** Three.js, GSAP, HyperFrames and their dependencies retain their respective licenses. See the packages for their license terms.

The original idea was prompted by [Joshua Barzon's timeline post](https://x.com/JoshuaBarzon/status/2119199745583591641). This credits the inspiring post, without claiming he authored the underlying historical chart.
